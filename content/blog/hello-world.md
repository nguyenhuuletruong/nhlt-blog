# Welcome to my Blog

This is where I share ideas, perspectives, and lessons from things I learn and experience.

Unlike my Digital Garden, where notes evolve over time, posts here are written as complete pieces that I want to share with others.

## What I write about

- **Product Management**: Building products, understanding users, and making better decisions.
- **Technology**: Exploring technologies, tools, and how they shape the way we work.
- **Investing**: Learning about businesses, valuation, and long-term investing.
- **Personal Knowledge Management**: Capturing, organizing, and connecting knowledge.
